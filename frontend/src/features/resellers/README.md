# Resellers

**Owner:** Member 1 — Products & Inventory (Robin Louis Putong)

## Requirements

- **FR-10 Reseller records:** business name, contact person, contact details, address, price tier, status, and data-privacy consent with its date (RA 10173).

## Scope notes

- These are internal records only. There is no reseller portal, login or invite e-mail.
- **Reseller orders** are recorded as sales with channel `reseller` in the Sales module (decision D3, SRS v1.1). There are no separate order screens.
